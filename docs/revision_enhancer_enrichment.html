Question

Do dynamic eQTLs discovered by FASH show orthogonal evidence of regulatory function? We tested whether one lead variant per discovered gene preferentially overlaps enhancer annotations from biologically relevant Roadmap epigenomes, using the Roadmap 15-state ChromHMM enhancer definition of states 6, 7 and 12 described by He and Wang (2017).

Section 1 compares the FASH and Strober discovery sets. Section 2 examines the FASH lead variants that neither Strober analysis identified.

The analysis is a descriptive, exploratory check, in six steps:

  1. take the published FASH and Strober discovery sets;
  2. keep one lead variant per discovered gene;
  3. define Roadmap enhancers as ChromHMM states 6, 7 and 12;
  4. record whether each variant overlaps each epigenome’s enhancer annotation;
  5. compare the overlap proportion with the remaining tested variants;
  6. estimate uncertainty with a leave-one-autosome-out jackknife.

Roadmap enhancer annotations

We used the Roadmap Epigenomics 15-state ChromHMM annotations (Roadmap Epigenomics Consortium et al. 2015) and defined enhancers as states 6_EnhG, 7_Enh and 12_EnhBiv (He and Wang 2017). Each of the 745,867 tested variants was assigned an overlap indicator for each of the thirteen epigenomes below.

FASH’s 1,169 lead variants show modest enrichment across several of the annotations: 7 of the 13 Roadmap annotations had pointwise jackknife intervals entirely above 1 (E021, E022, E011, E012, E013, E095, E105), spanning 1.25-1.51-fold. Both Strober lead sets point in broadly similar directions, with wider intervals that likely reflect in part their smaller discovery sets (548 and 690 lead variants). The pattern is spread across pluripotent, germ-layer and cardiac annotations, so it supports general regulatory enrichment rather than stage or tissue specificity. The three sets differ in size, membership and gene composition, and no formal statistical comparison between them is made here.

Section 2: FASH-specific discoveries

An rsID is counted as FASH-only if neither Strober analysis identified it, regardless of gene pairing. Lead selection happens first and the exclusion is applied afterwards, leaving 1,029 of FASH’s 1,169 lead variants. This is rsID-level exclusivity, not LD-independent locus-level novelty.

The enrichment estimates were similar after excluding rsIDs identified by either Strober analysis: 7 of the 13 annotations had pointwise intervals entirely above 1, spanning 1.24-1.61-fold against 1.25-1.51-fold for the unfiltered lead set. This gives orthogonal support for the regulatory relevance of the additional FASH discoveries as a set, rather than validating individual variants.

Methods

Discovery sets and lead variants. FASH uses cumulative lfdr at FDR 0.05 after the BF correction; both Strober analyses use their reported eFDR 0.05, with non_linear_dynamic_eqtls_5_pc.txt labelled quadratic/nonlinear. For each discovered gene we selected its strongest discovered variant — lowest lfdr (FASH) or smallest p-value (Strober) — and then evaluated the resulting unique lead variants, so a duplicated rsID counts once. A variant is FASH-only if neither Strober analysis reported that rsID.

Enhancer annotations. Roadmap Epigenomics 15-state ChromHMM segmentations for the thirteen epigenomes above, with enhancers defined as states 6_EnhG, 7_Enh and 12_EnhBiv (Roadmap Epigenomics Consortium et al. 2015; He and Wang 2017). Results were qualitatively similar using the broader enhancer-related definition (EnhG, Enh, EnhBiv and BivFlnk) implemented in the companion code for Strober et al. (2019) (fold enrichments correlated at 0.98 across the 51 cells estimable under both).

Fold enrichment. The proportion of selected variants overlapping the annotation divided by the same proportion among the remaining tested variants, which excludes the selected set from its own comparator. No matched controls are used. A pair with fewer than 10 overlapping selected variants is reported as not estimable.

Uncertainty. Uncertainty was assessed using a leave-one-autosome-out jackknife to account approximately for local dependence among variants. The intervals are pointwise, and the related Roadmap annotations should not be interpreted as independent tests; no multiplicity correction is applied.
