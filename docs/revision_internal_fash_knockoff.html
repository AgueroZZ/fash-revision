Internal exploratory page. This is an empirical calibration check, not a formal reviewer deliverable, and it is deliberately absent from analysis/_site.yml and analysis/index.Rmd. It does not claim finite-sample FDR control: exact target-decoy exchangeability is not established here, so the procedure is a knockoff-inspired permutation check rather than a knockoff procedure.

What the procedure does

For each of the tested gene-variant pairs a matched permuted decoy is built, and target and decoy units are fitted jointly, so a single empirical-Bayes prior scores both sides of every pair.

  1. One global donor permutation. A single donor row map is applied to the entire genotype matrix, G_pi = P G, shared by all 745,867 variants and all 16 time points. Row permutation leaves every variant’s dosage multiset, and therefore its MAF, exactly unchanged, and leaves the full LD structure unchanged because (PG)' (PG) = G' P' P G = G' G identically.
  2. The map is a derangement. A fixed point would leave one donor’s decoy genotype equal to its own, so that donor would keep feeding real signal into the decoy. The realised map is a 17-cycle plus a 2-cycle, with no fixed points.
  3. Expression PCs are not permuted. PC1-PC5 are functions of the expression matrix; permuting them relative to the expression would make the decoy regression a different model from the target regression.
  4. The original model is refit at each time point, Y ~ 1 + G_permuted + PC1 + ... + PC5, and the raw standard errors are converted to the same t-to-normal scale used by the primary analysis.
  5. Target likelihood rows are reused, never recomputed, from the published fit. BF_update() does not modify L_matrix, so those rows are bit-identical whichever fit is supplied. Only the decoy units required new likelihood evaluations.
  6. The statistic is W_j = log BF_j - log BF_j,pi, both Bayes factors scored by the merged prior. Selection uses knockoff+, T_q = min{t > 0 : (1 + #{W <= -t}) / (#{W >= t} v 1) <= q}.

Because the two sides share one prior, swapping a pair leaves the 2J-unit multiset unchanged, so the fitted prior is unchanged and only that pair’s W flips sign. This antisymmetry is asserted in test_fash_knockoff_helpers.R rather than assumed.

The decoy is not a simulated diagonal-Gaussian null. It is built from the same longitudinal expression measurements, so it retains the gene-specific temporal dependence induced by repeated measurements on the same cell lines. That is the point of the design, and, as the results below show, also the reason it has limited power.

Two things matter in this figure.

The non-discovered box is centred on zero. Median W is 0.00009 and W > 0 in 50.06% of the 999,922 pairs the published rule did not call. This is the strongest available check on the whole construction: on pairs with no claimed signal, target and decoy are behaving exchangeably. For contrast, earlier residual-block decoy constructions sat at 0.582 on a near-null pair set, about 13 sd from 0.5.

But the non-discovered tail reaches further than the discovered one. The decoy beats the target by as much as 28.4 log units among pairs that were never called. Those are pure artefact, because the decoy is null by construction, and they are what consumes the FDR budget.

This figure does not validate the calls. The grouping conditions on the target: the published rule selects pairs with a high target Bayes factor, and a high target BF mechanically forces W > 0 unless the decoy is also high. A null pair that reached a high BF by chance would sit in the same box. The figure locates W by discovery status; it is descriptive, not confirmatory. The same caveat applies to any statistic computed after conditioning on the published calls.

Does a different gene-level statistic help?

Any antisymmetric function of the two Bayes factors fixes the sign pattern, so only the ordering of |W| can change. Aggregating within a gene by a maximum is a difference of two extreme-value variables and is the noisiest choice.

  • The cached target likelihood rows reproduce exactly in the environment that computed the decoys: maximum absolute deviation 0 against a scale of 13.1, on a 50-unit slice. Target and decoy rows therefore sit on identical numerical footing.
  • The regression pipeline reproduces the published beta and SE from raw expression, PC and VCF inputs: maximum deviations 2.8e-13 and 1.6e-12 on a 2,000-pair slice. The decoy is built by the code path that reproduces the target.
  • The genotype-PC alignment gate is clean. Mean R^2 is 0.2847 for the observed genotype and 0.2863 for the permuted genotype, against a chance expectation near 5/18 = 0.278. Had the observed arm been systematically higher, decoy standard errors would have been deflated and the competition would have been anti-conservative in a way nothing downstream could reveal.
  • The informativeness screen dropped 23 variants and 37 pairs, 0.0037% of the total, all on the permuted arm and all at day 4. The screen is applied symmetrically to both arms and depends only on the design, never on an outcome; filtering on the decoy alone would break exchangeability.

Limitations

  • No claim of finite-sample FDR control. LD and shared-gene dependence across units are real, and the gene-level maximum only removes the within-gene part. This is an empirical calibration check.
  • A single permutation. One realisation, not an average, so accidental alignment at 19 donors cannot cancel. This is why the alignment gate above matters. Repeating with further seeds is a stability check, not an average.
  • Cell-line missingness is not specially handled. Three time points are incomplete: day 2 and day 4 have 16 donors, day 13 has 18. At those points the target uses the genotypes of the donors present while the decoy uses the genotypes of a different subset, so the two arms do not use the same dosage multiset. Measured, the decoy’s median residual genotype sum of squares is 0.9916 of the target’s at those three points against 0.9980 elsewhere: a roughly 1% asymmetry, in the conservative direction, at 3 of 16 time points. A permutation restricted to matching observation patterns would remove it, but the four patterns have sizes 13, 3, 2 and 1, so the singleton donor would be frozen and would leak real signal into the decoy for every variant. The current scheme is the lesser distortion, but it is an approximation.
  • Anything conditioned on the published calls is circular and is reported here only as description.
